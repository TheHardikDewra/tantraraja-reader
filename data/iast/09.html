kuryāccheṣāṇi cāṅgāni karayośca nyaset kramāt || 4 ||

nyasedaṅguṣṭhamūlādi kaniṣṭhāgrāntamūrdhvagam || 5 ||

śeṣaṃ taladvaye nyastvā hṛdvatstotreṇa sārddhayoḥ |
tvaci dhvaje ca pāyau ca pādayorvarṇakaṃ nyaset || 6 ||
